## How to Claim a Section

1. Open an issue titled `text: Claiming [Book] chapters [X-Y]`
2. Mark the section as `[~]` in this file via PR
3. Submit the text files when ready
4. Section will be marked `[x]` after verification

**Every chapter you add makes Torah more accessible to the world.**
